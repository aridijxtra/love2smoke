What shall I say? You smoke. And that is alright — when you want to, and do not harm others.

I know what it is to be a smoker. The question is not whether it is good or bad, but what you want: what kind of person do you really want to be?

Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 10, 2024

ADx respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you may provide while using our mobile application Love2Smoke (the "App").

Information we collect

User-generated data: the App may allow you to answer some multiple-choice questions. The answers to these questions are stored temporarily. This data remains within the App and is not shared or stored externally.

How we use your information

We do not use your personal information for any purpose, as we do not collect or store any personal information.

Data retention

User-generated data, including multiple-choice questions and answers, are only stored within the App and may be replaced when new data is entered by the user. We do not retain or access this data. When the App is deleted from the device, the data are deleted.

Sharing your information

We do not share your information with third parties because we do not collect or store personal information.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data within the App. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date" above. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
